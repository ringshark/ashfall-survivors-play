Ashfall SurvivorsField guide for new players

    Play the game

    Start here

    Ashfall Survivors is a top-down survival run. You pick a hero, fight through a maze of monsters, find the boss, and climb as many floors as you can. Everything you earn in a run is spent in the Town between floors.

    How a floor works

    • Every floor is a new maze with a 5:00 timer. If it reaches 0:00 the climb ends.
    • Kill monsters. At the kill goal (115 on floor 1, 145 on floor 2, 180 on floor 3, then 35 more each floor) the boss door opens.
    • The boss room is hidden. You find it by walking into it or getting near its door. Once the door is open, look for it.
    • Beat the boss. A chest drops where it fell and an exit portal appears. Take the chest, then step into the exit.
    • You land in the Town: heal, repair, shop, then walk through the Gate for the next floor. Your level, skills and build carry on.
    • If you fall, the climb ends. You keep your gear (it wears down), your scrap, Skill Points and records.

    Your basic attack fires by itself at the nearest monster. You steer, dodge and time your skills. Skills that need aiming (Shield Bash, Charge, Bone Wall and a few more) show an arrow while you hold the button and fire when you let go.

    Your first ten minutes

    • Collect the glowing XP gems. Each level-up gives you a choice of three cards.
    • Take a new skill early. Your first three level-ups always offer a new skill or a rank-up for one you have.
    • Use your potions. You get two per floor, and one press heals 30% of your max HP over 3 seconds.
    • Walls block arrows. Use corners as cover from archers.
    • Open the chests you find. They are the main way to get gear, but the dangerous ones wake monsters.
    • Your progress saves in your browser. Add the game to your home screen on a phone for a full-screen, app-like game.

    Heroes

    Six heroes, each with its own way to fight. All of them start with one active skill and earn the rest by levelling up and unlocking skills at the Skill Shrine.

    Hero names in game: Barbarian is Bigfoot, Necromancer is the Lich King, Mage is the Archmage, Ranger is the Elf, Animal Tamer is the Orc, Summoner is the Djinn.

    Skills and evolutions

    Each hero has six actives and seven passives. You start with one active. The Shrine sells the rest with Skill Points, and level-up cards put them in your hands.

    • You carry three actives at a time. Picking a fourth lets you swap one out; the swapped skill loses its ranks for that climb.
    • Actives rank up to 3. The capstone active (the last one on each list) only appears from level 8.
    • At its top rank, a skill can evolve once per climb into one of two forms. The evolve screen shows both; NOT YET asks again next floor.
    • Shrine prices: first two actives 1 point, next two 2 points, capstone 3 points, each passive 1 point. Unlocks are permanent for that hero.

    Floors and monsters

    The three realms

    FloorsRealmMonsters you will meet
    1 to 3Overgrown RuinsWarriors, spearmen, archers, runners
    4 to 6Sunken CryptAdds brutes and healers
    7 and upAsh FortressAdds bombers: all four special types

    Each floor picks one look for its ordinary monsters (goblin, hound, zombie, cultist or imp), so a floor feels like one tribe. The look is cosmetic.

    Monster types

    Warrior

    The common melee monster. Walks at you and hits on contact.

    Spearman

    Winds up, then charges in a straight line. Sidestep the charge.

    Archer

    Keeps its distance and shoots. Arrows stop at walls.

    Runner

    Fast, fragile, arrives in packs of three.

    Brute

    Slow and very tough. Resists knockback.

    Healer

    Heals other monsters around it in pulses and stays back. Kill it first.

    Bomber

    Runs at you and lights a fuse. Explodes when it dies, too, and hurts nearby monsters as well as you.

    The floor's element

    Every floor rolls one element (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth or Nature). Monsters take +30% damage from that element, and the floor shows a banner and a badge under the map. That element also brings floor hazards: fire vents, frost patches, lightning strikes, rockfall and spore pools. Hazards are telegraphed before they hurt.

    Imbue cards let any hero use the element. An Imbue card makes your basic attacks carry it: Fire burns, Ice chills, Nature poisons, Lightning sometimes jumps to another monster, Earth knocks back and sometimes stuns. If your imbue matches the floor's element, you also get the +30%. Taking a new Imbue card replaces the old one for the climb.

    Chests in the maze

    Floors hold two or three side chests. Gear from them is kept only if you finish the floor.

    ChestWhat happensReward
    PlainNothing. The nearest one.Mostly Common, sometimes Rare, rarely Epic. A little scrap.
    GuardedA pack of monsters wakes up around it.Rare or Epic, more scrap, and an extra reroll.
    CursedCalls a big wave.Always Epic, the most scrap, and an extra reroll.

    The minimap shows a chest once you have explored its area. Elites you have found show as red diamonds.

    Living floors

    Floors are not empty. Crates, barrels and urns stand in the corridors: break them by hitting monsters beside them. They sometimes hold gems, a bit of scrap or a small heal.

    Each floor has a shrine. Walk up to it for a blessing that lasts 30 seconds: Might (+20% damage), Warding (20% less damage taken) or Vigor (heals you a little every second). Each shrine works once.

    Moths, bats and rats scatter as you pass, and each floor has a few camps that match its monsters: goblin tents, hound bones, graves, cultist circles and imp slag pits. They are only scenery.

    In the Town the light follows your device's clock: bright by day, golden at dusk, blue at night with the windows and lanterns glowing.

    Elites and alcoves

    A few of each floor's monsters are elites: named, bigger, with a glowing ring, a health bar and one or two affixes that change how they fight. There are three on floor 1, one more every two floors, and never more than six. They sleep in a pack with their escorts until you come close or hit one, then the whole pack wakes. A banner tells you when one is near.

    Floors also hold a few alcoves: small dead-end rooms off the corridors. Each has a Vault chest (Rare or Epic gear) guarded by an elite pack.

    Stuns, roots and fear last half as long on an elite. A kill counts as ten kills and drops a Rare or Epic piece of gear (Rare most of the time), gems, a potion, scrap, and a little Mastery and Aspect XP. In co-op every player gets the reward.

    Bosses

    One boss waits on every floor, picked at random. All six hit hard on a telegraph you can dodge, and each drops a piece of its own gear set.

    Resist means the boss takes half damage from that element. Weak means it takes 1.5 times. Bring the element it is weak to and avoid the one it resists.
    • Most bosses have a concussion meter. Stuns and heavy hits fill it. When it fills, the boss is stunned for 2 seconds, then immune for 6.
    • Below 25% health most bosses enrage: faster, with more attacks.
    • Boss health and damage grow every floor you climb.
    • Once you have beaten a boss you can pick it as your target in the Trophy Hall, so every boss door brings that one.

    The Town

    A walled square between floors. Walk to a door and go in. The Town heals you, refills potions, and is where your gear and skills live.

    Blacksmith

    Repair worn gear with scrap (hammer taps restore durability), salvage unwanted pieces into scrap, and reforge Rare and Epic pieces to reroll their bonuses.

    Salvage by type scraps a whole slot and rarity in one go. It never touches worn pieces or set pieces and asks for a second tap.

    Armory

    Six slot tabs. Pick a piece to see what swapping would change, then EQUIP. Take Off clears a slot. SETS shows each set's bonuses and which ones you have reached.

    Skill Shrine

    Three pages: Skills (spend Skill Points on a hero's skills), Aspects and Mastery (long-term hero upgrades).

    Trophy Hall

    Your showroom. Set stands show every hero's pieces together, trophies count boss kills, and you can decorate. You can target a boss you have beaten here.

    The Gate

    Starts the next floor. It names the boss when you have set a target.

    Gear and sets

    Pieces

    Six slots: Helmet, Chest, Gloves, Legs, Boots and Weapon. Each slot has its own main stat (Helmet: crit and cooldowns, Chest: HP and armor, Gloves: attack speed, Legs: damage, Boots: move speed, Weapon: damage and crit damage).

    RarityWhat it adds
    CommonThe slot stat only
    RareSlot stat and one bonus stat
    EpicSlot stat, two bonus stats and one special effect (kills heal, a chance to stun, crits burn, or an extra potion each floor)
    Legendary (set)A boss set piece. Counts toward set bonuses.

    Durability

    Worn pieces lose 25% durability when a climb fails. At 0 a piece is broken and gives nothing until repaired. Finish your floors and you lose nothing.

    Boss chests

    A boss chest holds a Rare (62%), an Epic (35%) or a set piece (3%). There is a safety net: after seven boss chests from the same boss without a set piece for your hero, the eighth is guaranteed to be one.

    Set bonuses

    Worn, unbroken pieces count. Bonuses fix at the start of the floor. Set pieces found on any hero show on the Trophy Hall stands for all of them.

    Growing stronger

    Level-up cards

    • Each level shows three cards. At least one is always a plain buff, so you never have to drop a skill you like.
    • You get one reroll per floor, and more from guarded and cursed chests. You can also skip a level-up.
    • When every skill you could take is maxed, level-ups offer Ascension cards: strong bargains with a gain and a cost, taken once per climb.
    • The cards ignore touches for a moment after they appear, so you can read them first.

    Relics

    Beat a boss and you are offered three relics: small permanent stat boosts for the rest of the climb (some carry an Epic effect). A repeat relic ranks up to 3. The boss you just beat offers its own relics three times as often.

    Aspects

    A long-term upgrade tied to each boss. To unlock an Aspect, beat its boss three times (across all your heroes) and spend three Skill Points on the Aspects page. Each hero trains each Aspect separately by fighting with it equipped, and later tiers also cost scrap. An Aspect gives a flat stat and a signature effect, such as a ground slam, kills that heal, or an arc of lightning.

    Mastery

    Each hero has a skill tree with three lanes: Might (damage, crit, speed, cooldowns), Vitality (HP, armor, regen) and Wits (move speed, pickup range, XP). You earn Mastery points by playing. Each lane ends in a keystone: Executioner (extra damage to bosses), Last Stand (less damage taken when low) and Treasure Sense (every chest shown on the map). Respec for a little scrap.

    Records and the leaderboard

    The Records screen tracks your deepest floor, highest level and most kills for each hero. The BOARD shows the top climbs for everyone. Your best climb is sent automatically; you can pick your own display name on the board screen. Co-op runs and event runs are not sent.

    Battleground

    Team PvP, from the PLAY WITH FRIENDS lobby. Two teams fight over three Beacons on one arena, the Ashen Pits. Matches last at most five minutes.

    How to start

    • The host taps PLAY WITH FRIENDS, then HOST A ROOM, then the MODE button to switch CO-OP to BATTLEGROUND.
    • Friends join with the room code. Teams fill evenly (Blue and Red); tap TEAM to switch if the other side has room. Two to eight players.
    • Everyone taps READY and the host taps START.

    The rules

    • Beacons. Stand inside a ring alone to capture it (about 8 seconds). Enemies in the ring freeze it. Each Beacon you hold scores 1 point a second.
    • Kills. Defeating an enemy hero scores 5 points, and 5 more if they were standing in a Beacon ring.
    • Winning. First team to 100 points, or the higher score at 5:00. A tie goes to the team holding more Beacons, otherwise it is a draw.
    • Bases. Your base is a safe zone: nobody can hit you there, and you cannot be targeted. You come back to it 6 seconds after going down, with 2 seconds of protection.
    • Your build counts. Your gear, set bonuses, Mastery and Aspects carry over. Everyone is level 10 with a fixed three-skill kit for their hero, health is doubled, and hero-on-hero damage is scaled down so fights last.
    • Crowd control is shortened and cannot be chained: after being stunned or rooted you are immune for 2 seconds.
    • Skeletons, Beasts and summons fight too. Matches are saved only as wins, losses and kills on the Records screen.

    Events

    The Hollow Harvest

    A limited Halloween event, open from 21 October to 2 November 2026. Everything you unlock stays after it ends.

    Start it from the event page on the title screen. A Hollow Run is three floors under a harvest moon, with smashable pumpkins that drop candy (healing and bonus XP), and extra chests. The last floor always ends with Gourdon, the Pumpkin King.
